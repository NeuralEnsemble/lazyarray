==========
Governance
==========

lazyarray is a small, community-developed Python library hosted under the
`NeuralEnsemble organisation <https://github.com/NeuralEnsemble>`_ on
GitHub. It welcomes contributions from anyone who is interested in the
project. All contributors agree to abide by the
`Code of Conduct <https://github.com/NeuralEnsemble/lazyarray/blob/master/CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md>`_.

Maintainers
===========

The current active maintainer is:

* Andrew P. Davison (CNRS / NeuroPSI)

Past authors and contributors are listed in the ``AUTHORS`` file at the
root of the repository.

Any contributor who has had at least three pull requests accepted may be
nominated as a maintainer. Nominations must be approved by a majority of the
existing maintainer(s), with no dissenting maintainer.

Contributions
=============

All non-trivial contributions are made by pull request against the
``master`` branch, with the exception of quick bug fixes affecting fewer
than ten lines of code, which a maintainer may push directly.

Pull requests may be approved by any maintainer; anyone is welcome to
join the discussion. In case of disagreement, the maintainers will try to
reach consensus, taking account of any input from the wider community.
If consensus cannot be reached, decisions will be made by majority vote
of the maintainers.

Code review checks for:

* tests covering new or changed behaviour;
* a corresponding changelog entry;
* no regressions in the CI matrix;
* adherence to the :doc:`coding style <contributing>`.

Decision-making
===============

Day-to-day decisions are made by the maintainers. Larger questions, such as
breaking API changes, are discussed publicly on the
`NeuralEnsemble Google Group <http://groups.google.com/group/neuralensemble>`_
or in a GitHub issue, with a reasonable opportunity for community input
before a decision is taken.
